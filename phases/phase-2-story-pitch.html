The pitch

A pitch is you making the case: what the story is, why it earns a reader's attention, and how you'll report it fairly. The Coordinator approves it before you collect a single price. Be specific — the sourcing and reasoning fields want your actual plan, not placeholders.
Working headline
The hook (1–2 sentences)

Why does a reader click this today? Lead with the sharpest reason.

Thesis / hypothesis

What do you expect to find, and why — e.g., "natural-food stores run roughly double the discount chains on the same basket." Write it down now. You'll test it, not assume it; and when the real numbers land, the gap between what you predicted and what you found is often the most honest line in the piece.
